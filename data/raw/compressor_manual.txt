COMPRESSOR OPERATING MANUAL

Section 1: Normal Operation

The compressor should normally operate between 60 and 80 bar.
The recommended operating pressure under standard load conditions
is between 65 and 75 bar.

Section 2: Temperature

The bearing temperature should normally remain below 80°C.
If the bearing temperature exceeds 85°C, the operator should
inspect the lubrication system.

Section 3: Emergency Conditions

If compressor pressure exceeds 85 bar, initiate the emergency
shutdown procedure and inspect the pressure control system.
